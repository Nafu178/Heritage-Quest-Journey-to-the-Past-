# Heritage Quest

Heritage Quest is a cozy 2D adventure game where players travel back to prehistoric times to explore the world of early humans and Malaysian heritage. Explore caves, forests, and villages while solving puzzles, completing quests, and uncovering stories from the past. 🎮✨

## Features
- 2D top-down adventure gameplay
- Puzzle-solving and quest system
- Dialogue and cutscenes
- Educational storytelling about early humans
- Sound effects and immersive atmosphere
- Simple and relaxing gameplay experience

## Gameplay
Players explore different prehistoric areas, interact with NPCs, solve challenges, and learn historical facts naturally through gameplay and storytelling.
